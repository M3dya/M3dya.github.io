    Why priority is published, not purchased: Ontario families told us the biggest source of mistrust is not knowing why one child gets a spot over another. So priority on DaycareConnect can only come from a fixed, visible list of reasons — a sibling already enrolled, CWELCC subsidy eligibility, a documented medical or circumstantial need, or a staff child — the same categories used in Quebec's provincial system. You can apply a tag to a family with a short reason, but the categories can't be created or sold, and every family can see the rules that apply to everyone.
